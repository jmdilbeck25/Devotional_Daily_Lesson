Grace Beyond Every Limit

"Jesus answered, 'I tell you, not seven times, but seventy-seven times.'" — Matthew 18:22

Peter asked Jesus a very practical question about forgiveness, assuming that seven times was a generous limit. But Jesus completely reframed the conversation. By telling Peter to forgive seventy-seven times, Jesus was not giving a new mathematical limit to track offenses. He was revealing that God's mercy does not operate on a ledger system.

When you choose to forgive someone who has hurt you, it can feel exhausting and unfair. Yet Jesus reminds us that our capacity to forgive flows directly from the bottomless well of grace we have received from God. Living in His love means letting go of the scorekeeping that keeps us trapped in bitterness.

Living It Out

Today, identify one person against whom you are holding a grudge or keeping score. Consciously choose to release that offense in your heart, trusting God to give you the strength to move forward in grace.

Prayer

Father, thank You for forgiving me countless times without keeping track of my failures. Help me to let go of lingering resentment and offer that same mercy to others today. Fill my heart with Your boundless love so I can walk in true freedom. Amen.
