The Endless Gift of Forgiveness

"Jesus answered, 'I tell you, not seven times, but seventy-seven times.'" — Matthew 18:22

Peter thought he was being generous when he offered to forgive someone seven times. In his culture, forgiving three times was considered ample. But Jesus flipped his expectations upside down. By telling Peter to forgive seventy-seven times, Jesus was not giving us a mathematical formula to track offenses. He was revealing the limitless, unconditional nature of God's grace toward us.

When you choose to forgive someone who has hurt you, you reflect the very heart of Jesus. Holding onto resentment only heavy-chains your own soul. Real forgiveness isn't about ignoring the pain or pretending what happened was okay; it is about choosing to release the debt because Christ has freely released yours.

Living It Out

Take a quiet moment today to bring to mind someone you have been holding a grudge against. Pray for that person by name, and consciously choose to hand that burden over to God, releasing your right to get even.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank You for forgiving my mistakes over and over again without keeping count. Help me to show that same radical mercy to others today, letting go of my grudges and choosing peace instead. Amen.
