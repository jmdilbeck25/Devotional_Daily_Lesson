Forgiving Without Keeping Score

"Jesus answered, 'I tell you, not seven times, but seventy-seven times.'" — Matthew 18:22

Peter thought he was being remarkably generous when he asked if he should forgive someone seven times. In a culture where forgiving three times was considered ample, seven felt like the peak of patience. But Jesus completely changed the conversation. By telling Peter to forgive seventy-seven times, He was not giving us a higher math limit to track. Instead, He was showing that true love stops keeping count altogether.

This kind of endless forgiveness reflects the overwhelming mercy God extends to you every single day. When you stop tallying the mistakes of others, you set yourself free from the heavy weight of resentment. God does not run out of grace for you when you stumble, and He invites you to offer that same open-handed freedom to the people in your life.

Living It Out

Think of one person who has recently irritated you or caused you hurt, even in a small way. Make a conscious choice today to release that mental scorecard and let go of the offense. Pray a brief, sincere blessing over them, choosing to extend the same fresh start you receive from God.

Prayer

Lord, thank You for the endless patience and forgiveness You show me each day. Help me to drop my mental scorecards and extend that same grace to others. Give me a soft heart that chooses love over resentment. Amen.
