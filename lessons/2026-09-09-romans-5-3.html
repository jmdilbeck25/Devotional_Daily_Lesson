Finding Purpose in Our Struggles

"Not only so, but we also glory in our sufferings, because we know that suffering produces perseverance;" — Romans 5:3

Life often brings unexpected hardship that can leave you feeling weary and overwhelmed. When Paul speaks about finding purpose in suffering, it can sound almost impossible at first. Yet God does not ask you to pretend that pain feels good, but rather to trust that He can work through it to shape your heart.

Suffering has a unique way of stripping away self-reliance and drawing you closer to God. As you walk through difficult seasons with Him, your endurance grows stronger step by step. The perseverance you build today becomes the foundation for a deep, unshakable faith tomorrow.

Living It Out

Today, take a moment to identify one current struggle in your life and intentionally hand it over to God. Ask Him to help you focus not just on the relief of ending the trial, but on the patience and strength He wants to build inside you right now.

Prayer

Lord, thank You for standing beside me in every difficult moment. Help me to trust that You are using my current challenges to build endurance and draw me closer to You. Give me the strength to keep moving forward in faith today. Amen.
