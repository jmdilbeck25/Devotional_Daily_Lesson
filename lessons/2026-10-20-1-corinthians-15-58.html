Your Labor Is Never Wasted

"Therefore, my dear brothers and sisters, stand firm. Let nothing move you. Always give yourselves fully to the work of the Lord, because you know that your labor in the Lord is not in vain." — 1 Corinthians 15:58

Life often feels exhausting, especially when you pour your heart into caring for others, serving your community, or doing what is right without seeing immediate results. It is easy to wonder if your quiet acts of kindness and daily faithfulness actually make a difference. In this passage, Paul grounds our daily perseverance in the truth of Christ's resurrection.

Because Jesus has secured the ultimate victory, nothing you do for Him is lost or forgotten. Even the smallest, most ordinary tasks carry eternal weight when done out of love for God. You can stand unshakeable today, knowing that God sees every sacrifice and brings lasting value to all your labor.

Living It Out

Pick one routine responsibility or act of service today that feels unappreciated or tiring, and quietly pray over it, offering it to God as a joyful act of worship.

Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for reminding me that my efforts in You are never wasted. Give me the strength to stand firm today and serve others with a refreshed heart. Help me trust that You see everything I do for Your kingdom. Amen.
