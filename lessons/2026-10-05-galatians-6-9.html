Sowing Seeds of Patient Faith

"Let us not become weary in doing good, for at the proper time we will reap a harvest if we do not give up." — Galatians 6:9

It is easy to feel exhausted when you continuously invest love, kindness, and effort into others without seeing immediate results. Doing good can often feel like planting seeds in hard soil, where day after day passes with no visible sign of growth or reward. Paul acknowledges this real human fatigue and gently reminds us that feeling weary is a natural struggle we all face.

Yet God promises that no act of goodness done in His name is ever wasted. Spiritual growth and heart transformation take time, just like a physical harvest. When you stay faithful in the quiet, unseen moments of obedience, God is actively working beneath the surface to bring about a fruitful result at just the right time.

Living It Out

Today, choose one specific act of kindness or service that has felt thankless or tiring lately, and do it again with a refreshed heart. Whether it is sending an encouraging text, listening patiently to a friend, or quietly completing a household task, do it with the confidence that God sees your faithful effort.

Prayer

Lord, give me the strength to keep choosing what is good even when I feel tired or unappreciated. Help me trust Your perfect timing for the harvest ahead. Fill my heart with patience and joy as I serve You today. Amen.
