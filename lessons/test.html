Trusting the Path Ahead

"Trust in the LORD with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways submit to him, and he will make your paths straight." — Proverbs 3:5-6

Sometimes the next step isn't completely clear, but faith doesn't require seeing the entire staircase—just taking the first step in obedience.

Today, focus on surrendering control over the things you cannot change and trusting that God is directing your steps every day.
