Called to Share His Love

"Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit." — Matthew 28:19

Jesus gave His followers a breathtaking commission right before He ascended into heaven. He did not tell them to stay comfortable in their familiar routines, but to go out into the world and share the good news. This call was not just meant for scholars or famous preachers; it was given to ordinary people just like you.

Making disciples sounds like a huge task, but at its heart, it is simply about inviting others to experience the grace and truth you have found in God. As you step out in obedience, you do not go alone. You carry the loving presence of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit with you into every room you enter today.

Living It Out

Today, look for one small opportunity to reflect Christ's love to someone in your daily life. It could be offering a genuine word of encouragement to a coworker, listening patiently to a friend in need, or sharing a simple story of how God has helped you recently.

Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for inviting me to be a part of Your work in the world. Give me the courage to share Your love with those around me today. Please guide my steps and let Your peace flow through me to everyone I meet. Amen.
