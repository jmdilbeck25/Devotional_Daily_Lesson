Sharpening One Another in Faith

"As iron sharpens iron, so one man sharpens another." — Proverbs 27:17

When two iron blades rub together, the process is neither soft nor quiet. It involves friction, contact, and a little bit of heat. Yet, this intentional rubbing is precisely what removes dullness and restores a keen, useful edge. In the same way, God never designed you to walk through life alone. Growth happens when your life rubs shoulders with the lives of other believers who love you enough to speak truth into your heart.\n\nStaying isolated might feel safe and comfortable, but over time, it leaves your faith feeling dull and unprepared for life's challenges. Healthy Christian friendships bring encouragement, gentle challenge, and honest accountability. When you allow trusted friends to share your journey, their wisdom and faith help refine your own, making both of you stronger and more effective for God's kingdom.

Living It Out

Today, take a practical step toward intentional community by reaching out to a trusted Christian friend or mentor. Send them a text or invite them for coffee, and share one specific area in your life where you could use prayer and honest encouragement.

Prayer

Father, thank you for placing people in my life who help me grow closer to you. Give me the courage to open up, be vulnerable, and allow others to encourage and sharpen my faith. Help me to also be a faithful, supportive friend to those around me today. Amen.
