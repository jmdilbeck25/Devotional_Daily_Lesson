DAILY DEVOTIONAL

Uniquely Designed by God's Love

"I praise you because I am fearfully and wonderfully made; your works are wonderful, I know that full well." — Psalm 139:14

It is easy to look in the mirror and focus on your flaws, comparing yourself to others or wishing you were different. But God looks at you with complete affection and deliberate intention. Before you took your first breath, He crafted your personality, your gifts, and your very being with divine precision. You are not an accident or a product of chance; you are the intentional artwork of a loving Creator.

When the psalmist says you are fearfully and wonderfully made, it means God put awe-inspiring care into creating you. Recognizing this truth is not about pride, but about giving glory back to the One who made you. Knowing full well that God's works are wonderful allows you to walk with quiet confidence, resting in the value He has already placed on your life.

Living It Out

Today, whenever a self-critical thought enters your mind, pause and turn it into a quiet thank you to God. Take a moment to name three specific traits or abilities He gave you, and thank Him for designing you just as you are.

Prayer

Lord, thank You for creating me with such intentional care and love. Help me to see myself through Your eyes instead of comparing myself to the world around me. May my life bring praise to You as I rest in Your wonderful design. Amen.
