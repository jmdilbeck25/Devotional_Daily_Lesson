Courage for Your Next Step

"The following night the Lord stood near Paul and said, 'Take courage! As you have testified about me in Jerusalem, so you must also testify in Rome.'" — Acts 23:11

Paul had just endured a terrifying, chaotic day in Jerusalem. Trapped in a barracks and facing an uncertain future, he was likely exhausted and drained. But in the quiet of the night, Jesus did not send a distant message; He came and stood right beside Paul. The Lord acknowledged what Paul had already faced and gently assured him that his story was far from over.

You might be coming off a difficult season or feeling anxious about what lies ahead. In those heavy moments, it is easy to wonder if your current struggle is the end of the line. But the same Lord who drew near to Paul stands beside you today. He sees every faithful effort you have made, comforts your weary heart, and promises that He still has a meaningful purpose for your future.

Living It Out

Today, set aside five quiet minutes to identify one specific fear or uncertainty hanging over your head. Write it on a piece of paper, and right beside it, write the promise: The Lord stands near. Hand that burden over to Him in prayer and take your next small step in faith.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank You for standing right beside me even when I feel weary or afraid. Grant me the strength to trust Your presence and the courage to follow where You lead. Remind me today that my story rests safely in Your hands. Amen.
