Tending the Garden of Today

"The Lord God took the man and put him in the Garden of Eden to work it and take care of it." — Genesis 2:15

Long before work felt exhausting or stressful, God gave humanity a job. In the quiet beauty of Eden, God placed Adam in the garden not to sit idle, but to cultivate and protect it. This shows us that meaningful work is not a burden; it is part of God's original, beautiful design for your life. Your daily effort has holy roots.

God calls you to tend to whatever space He has placed you in right now. Whether you are managing a household, working at a desk, caring for a family member, or serving in your community, your work matters to Him. When you care for what has been entrusted to you, you reflect the heart of the Creator who lovingly cares for all He made.

Living It Out

Choose one task today that usually feels routine or burdensome, and approach it as an act of service to God. As you complete it, remind yourself that you are tending a small piece of God's world, bringing order and care to the place He has assigned to you.

Prayer

Lord, thank You for placing me where I am today and giving my work real purpose. Help me to care for the tasks and people in my life with patience, diligence, and joy. Teach me to see my daily routine as a way to serve You. Amen.
