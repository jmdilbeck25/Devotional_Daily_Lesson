Stepping Into Courageous Faith

"I am about to go the way of all the earth. Be strong, therefore, and show yourself a man." — 1 Kings 2:2

David was at the end of his earthly journey, handing the mantle of leadership to his young son Solomon. He knew that life brings transitions, some of which feel overwhelming or far beyond our abilities. Rather than giving Solomon a complex checklist, David pointed him straight to the heart of the matter: the need for quiet, rooted strength in God.

When life calls you to step into new responsibilities or face difficult seasons, it is natural to feel unprepared. Yet courage is not about feeling fearless or having everything figured out. True strength comes from trusting that God goes before you, giving you the grace to stand tall, act faithfully, and honor Him in whatever lies ahead.

Living It Out

Take five minutes today to identify one area of your life where you feel hesitant or inadequate. Speak a simple statement of trust over that situation, and take one brave, concrete step forward today.

Prayer

Lord, thank You for holding my future and giving me strength when I feel weak. Help me to stand firm in faith today and step boldly into the work You have placed before me.
