The Power of Wise Company

"Walk with the wise and become wise, for a companion of fools suffers harm." — Proverbs 13:20

The people you spend time with quietly shape the person you are becoming. It rarely happens all at once. Instead, through daily conversations, shared values, and subtle influences, you begin to adopt the habits and attitudes of your closest friends. Proverbs reminds us that wisdom is contagious, but so is folly. When you walk alongside those who love truth and seek God, their character naturally rubs off on you.

On the other hand, staying close to reckless choices and toxic behavior eventually takes a toll on your peace and integrity. This is not about cutting people off out of pride or judgment, but about being intentional with who shapes your heart. God desires to surround you with companions who encourage your faith, speak light into your struggles, and help you grow every day.

Living It Out

Take a moment today to reflect on your inner circle and reach out to one wise, encouraging friend. Send them a quick text to thank them for their positive influence in your life, or set up a time to catch up.

Prayer

Lord, thank You for placing wise and faithful people in my path. Give me the discernment to choose companions who honor You, and help me to be a supportive, wise friend to others. Amen.
