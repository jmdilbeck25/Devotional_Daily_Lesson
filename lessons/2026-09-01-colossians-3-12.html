Clothed in God's Deep Love

"Therefore, as God’s chosen people, holy and dearly loved, clothe yourselves with compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness and patience." — Colossians 3:12

Before Paul tells you what to do, he reminds you who you are. You are chosen, holy, and dearly loved by God. Your identity is not defined by your mistakes, your stress, or the opinions of others. You are set apart and cherished by your Creator. When you truly grasp how deeply God loves you, your heart naturally shifts from self-protection to grace toward others.

Paul uses the picture of getting dressed to describe your daily walk with God. Just as you choose your clothes each morning, you can consciously choose to put on compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness, and patience. These virtues are not things you must manufacture in your own strength. They are the character of Jesus, given to you as a gift to wear into the world today.

Living It Out

Today, pay attention to the moments when frustration or impatience starts to rise up within you. Before responding to a difficult person or a stressful situation, pause for a single breath and intentionally choose kindness or gentleness instead.

Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for calling me chosen and dearly loved. Help me to put on Your kindness and patience today as I interact with the people around me. May my heart reflect Your gentle grace to the world. Amen.
