DAILY DEVOTIONAL

Asking the Generous Giver

"If any of you lacks wisdom, you should ask God, who gives generously to all without finding fault, and it will be given to you." — James 1:5

We often face decisions that leave us feeling overwhelmed or unsure of the right path forward. Whether you are navigating a strained relationship, a tough career choice, or simply trying to honor God in your daily routine, clarity can feel hard to find. The good news of James 1:5 is that you do not have to figure everything out on your own. God does not expect you to possess all the answers before you come to Him.

When you ask God for guidance, He does not look down on you for your confusion or criticize you for not knowing what to do. Instead, He delights in giving wisdom freely and abundantly to anyone who asks. You can approach Him with complete confidence, knowing that His heart toward you is filled with grace, patience, and generosity.

Living It Out

Today, take five minutes to bring one specific point of confusion or a tough decision to God. Before searching for answers elsewhere, ask Him directly for His wisdom and trust Him to lead your next step.

Prayer

Heavenly Father, I admit that I do not always know the right path forward. Thank You for promising to give me wisdom generously without scolding me for my lack of understanding. Please guide my heart and mind as I make decisions today. Amen.
