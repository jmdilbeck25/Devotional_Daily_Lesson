Giving God Your First Best

"Honor the Lord with your wealth, with the firstfruits of all your crops;" — Proverbs 3:9

In ancient times, the firstfruits were the very first harvest of a crop. Offering them to God was an act of deep trust, thanking Him before knowing how the rest of the season would unfold. God did not ask for leftover scraps; He invited His people to put Him first in their practical, everyday lives.

Today, honoring God with your wealth works the same way. It is a joyful declaration that everything you have comes from His loving hands. When you choose to give Him your best right at the start, you train your heart to rely on His faithful provision rather than your own security.

Living It Out

Look at your income or budget today and find one practical way to put God first. Whether it means giving to your local church, supporting a neighbor in need, or setting aside a dedicated portion of your next paycheck, choose to honor Him proactively before paying for extra comforts.

Prayer

Lord, thank You for generously providing for all of my daily needs. Help me to trust You completely and honor You with the best of what I have received. Teach my heart to give with real joy and gratitude today. Amen.
