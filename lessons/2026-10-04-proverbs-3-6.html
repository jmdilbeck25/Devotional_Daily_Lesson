Trusting God with Every Step

"in all your ways submit to him, and he will make your paths straight." — Proverbs 3:6

It is easy to invite God into the big decisions of life, like choosing a career or buying a home, while keeping the smaller, everyday routines to ourselves. Yet, Proverbs 3:6 encourages you to involve Him in all your ways. True trust means opening every corner of your life to God's guidance, acknowledging His wisdom in your work, your relationships, and your quiet moments.

When you yield control to God, He promises to direct your steps and clear away the confusion. Making your paths straight does not mean a life free of trouble, but rather a journey filled with clarity and divine purpose. As you surrender your plans to Him today, you can rest in the comfort that God is leading you exactly where you need to go.

Living It Out

Take a moment right now to identify one specific area of your day that you have been trying to manage entirely on your own. Consciously surrender that situation to God, asking Him to give you wisdom and clear direction as you navigate it today.

Prayer

Lord, I surrender my plans, decisions, and worries into Your loving hands today. Help me to acknowledge You in everything I do and trust that You are guiding my steps. Make my path clear as I follow You. Amen.
