Walking Beside the Wise

"Walk with the wise and become wise, for a companion of fools suffers harm." — Proverbs 13:20

Look around at the people you spend the most time with today. Whether you realize it or not, their habits, attitudes, and values are quietly shaping your perspective. God designed us for community, and the relationships we choose to nurture have a deep, lasting influence on our daily character and direction.

This proverb reminds us that wisdom is caught as much as it is taught. When you walk alongside people who love truth, practice patience, and honor God, their wisdom becomes contagious. Choosing wise companions helps guard your heart and keeps your feet on a path of peace and purpose.

Living It Out

Take a quiet moment today to evaluate your closest relationships. Reach out to one person whose faith and godly character you admire, and set up a time to grab coffee, take a walk, or catch up on the phone.

Prayer

Lord, thank You for the gift of faithful community. Please give me wisdom to choose companions who draw me closer to You. Help me also to be a wise, encouraging friend to everyone I meet today. Amen.
