Finding Courage in God Spirit

"For God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power and of love and of a sound mind." — 2 Timothy 1:7

Fear has a sneaky way of shrinking your world. It tells you that you are not enough, that the future is unsafe, or that you should retreat into self-protection. But Paul reminds us that fear is not a gift from God. When anxiety begins to take root in your heart, you can be certain that it is not the voice of your loving Father guiding you.

Instead, God has filled you with His Holy Spirit, equipping you with power, love, and a sound mind. His power strengthens you when you feel weak. His love anchors your heart and casts out worry. His gift of a sound mind brings clarity, peace, and self-control when life feels chaotic. You do not have to live held hostage by fear, because God has already given you everything you need to stand firm.

Living It Out

Today, when you notice fear or anxiety trying to dictate your choices, pause and take a deep breath. Quietly recite this verse and choose one small step of action based on trust and love rather than fear.

Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for giving me Your Holy Spirit filled with power, love, and a sound mind. Whenever fear creeps into my day, help me to lean into Your strength and rest in Your perfect love. Guide my mind with Your peace so I can walk forward in confidence. Amen.
