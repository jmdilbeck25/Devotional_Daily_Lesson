DAILY DEVOTIONAL

We Were Never Meant Alone

"If either of them falls down, one can help the other up. But pity anyone who falls and has no one to help them up." — Ecclesiastes 4:10

Life has a way of catching us off guard. Whether it is a sudden loss, an overwhelming season of anxiety, or a quiet failure that leaves us feeling ashamed, everyone experiences moments of falling down. In those moments, trying to push through in isolation only deepens our pain. God knows our limitations and never intended for you to carry the heavy burdens of this life completely by yourself.

He designed us for community so that when one person stumbles, another is nearby to offer a hand. Reaching out for support or allowing someone to help you is not a sign of weakness; it is actually how God delivers His comfort and strength to you. Surrounding yourself with faithful friends ensures that when life trips you up, you are never left stranded in the dust.

Living It Out

Today, take one intentional step toward authentic community. Reach out to a trusted Christian friend to share an honest update about how you are doing, or offer a listening ear and a helping hand to someone you know is going through a tough season.

Prayer

Lord, thank You for placing people in my life who support me when I stumble. Give me the humility to reach out when I am struggling, and grant me the grace to lift others up when they fall. Amen.
