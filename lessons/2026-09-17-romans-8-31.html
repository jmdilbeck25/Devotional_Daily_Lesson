God Is Always On Your Side

"What, then, shall we say in response to these things? If God is for us, who can be against us?" — Romans 8:31

Life often brings moments where it feels like challenges are piling up against you. Whether it is a difficult situation or quiet self-doubt, opposition can make you feel small and overwhelmed. But Paul reminds us of a foundational truth: the Creator of the universe is actively on your side.

When Scripture asks who can be against us, it does not promise a life free from trouble. Instead, it assures you that no circumstance or fear is stronger than the God who loves you. His presence surrounds you, and His grace ensures that nothing standing against you has the final word.

Living It Out

Today, whenever you feel a surge of worry or face a daunting moment, pause for a breath and whisper, 'God is for me.' Let this truth quiet your mind, restore your courage, and guide your decisions with steady peace.

Prayer

Lord, thank You for standing with me and holding me secure through every challenge. Remind me today that Your love is infinitely stronger than any fear or obstacle I face. Amen.
