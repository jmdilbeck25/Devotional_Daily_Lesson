What God Asks of You

"He has shown you, O mortal, what is good. And what does the Lord require of you? To act justly and to love mercy and to walk humbly with your God." — Micah 6:8

Life often feels overwhelming with endless expectations and choices. We can easily wonder what God truly wants from us in the middle of our busy days. In this verse, God cuts through the noise and offers a clear, beautiful blueprint for living. He does not demand impossible perfection or complex rituals, but simply calls you to reflect His loving heart.

To act justly means treating people with fairness and honesty. To love mercy means offering forgiveness and gentle kindness generously. To walk humbly with God means depending on His strength rather than relying on your own. When you embrace these three simple invitations, your daily life becomes a quiet testament to His grace.

Living It Out

Look for one opportunity today to extend unexpected mercy to someone around you, whether by offering a kind word to a coworker, forgiving a small frustration, or helping a neighbor in need.

Prayer

Lord, thank You for showing me what is good and keeping Your path simple. Teach me to treat others fairly, love them mercifully, and walk humbly with You every step of today. Amen.
