Extending the Grace You Received

"Be kind and compassionate to one another, forgiving each other, just as in Christ God forgave you." — Ephesians 4:32

In a world that often rewards holding grudges and keeping score, Paul offers a radically different way to live. Kindness and compassion are not just passive feelings; they are active choices to view others through the lens of mercy. When someone hurts or disappoints you, your natural reaction might be to pull away or retaliate. Yet, God calls you to lean in with a tender heart, choosing empathy over bitterness.

The key to living this out lies in remembering how God treats you. You do not offer forgiveness because the other person necessarily earned it, but because you have already received an overflowing abundance of unearned grace in Christ. Reminding yourself of how deeply you are loved and forgiven by God frees your heart to release the offenses of others.

Living It Out

Today, think of one person toward whom you have been holding frustration or resentment. Take one concrete step to show them kindness, whether by sending a warm text message, praying for their well-being, or consciously choosing in your heart to let go of the offense.

Prayer

Lord, thank You for forgiving me so completely through Christ. Soften my heart today so that I can show tender compassion to those around me. Help me to love and forgive others just as You have generously forgiven me. Amen.
