Loving God with Wholehearted Devotion

"He answered, 'Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your strength and with all your mind'; and, 'Love your neighbor as yourself.'" — Luke 10:27

Jesus points us to the very core of what it means to follow Him. Loving God is not just an emotion or a Sunday routine; it engages every part of who you are. Your emotions, your spiritual depth, your physical energy, and your intellect are all invited into this deep relationship with your Creator. God desires all of you, not just a small fraction of your life.

Notice how this love naturally flows outward toward the people around you. Loving your neighbor is not a separate assignment, but the natural result of a heart filled with God's grace. When you truly experience God's love, it softens your heart, allowing you to show genuine kindness, patience, and compassion to those you meet every day.

Living It Out

Take a moment today to pause and reflect on how you can share God's love in a practical way. Choose one concrete action to bless someone around you, whether that means sending an encouraging text message to a friend, offering a listening ear to a colleague, or helping a neighbor with a daily chore.

Prayer

Lord, help me to love You today with all my heart, soul, mind, and strength. Open my eyes to see my neighbors as You see them, and give me the grace to serve them well. Amen.
