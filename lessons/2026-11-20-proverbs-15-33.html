The Pathway to True Honor

"Wisdom’s instruction is to fear the Lord, and humility comes before honor." — Proverbs 15:33

We live in a culture that constantly urges us to promote ourselves, push to the front of the line, and demand recognition. Yet God offers us a radically different path to greatness. True wisdom begins not with self-reliance, but with a deep, reverent awe of the Lord. When you honor Him as the rightful King of your life, everything else falls into its proper place.

Humility is not thinking less of yourself, but choosing to trust God with your reputation and your future. Before God lifts you up with true, lasting honor, He gently invites you to bow your heart in quiet surrender. Real honor is never something you have to force or grab for yourself; it is a grace God delights to bestow on the humble.

Living It Out

Today, look for one intentional opportunity to step back and let someone else shine. Whether it means giving up the last word in a conversation, letting a teammate take the credit, or serving behind the scenes without needing acknowledgment, practice choosing humility in a moment where you would normally seek recognition.

Prayer

Lord, help me to walk in reverent awe of who You are today. Teach my heart true humility so that I seek Your approval above the applause of others. Guide my words and actions so that You receive all the glory. Amen.
