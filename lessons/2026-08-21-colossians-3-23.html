DAILY DEVOTIONAL

Working for a Higher Purpose

"Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters." — Colossians 3:23

It is easy to feel like your daily routines are repetitive or unimportant, especially when your hard work goes unnoticed by bosses, clients, or family members. You might naturally measure the value of your labor by the recognition, paycheck, or praise you receive from others. But Paul turns this perspective upside down by reminding you that your ultimate audience is God Himself.

When you realize that every task—from filing reports to washing dishes—can be an act of devotion, ordinary moments become deeply meaningful. You are no longer serving just an earthly supervisor or trying to keep up with expectations; you are offering your effort directly to the Lord who loves you.

Living It Out

Pick one routine task you usually dread today, pause for a moment before you start it, and intentionally offer your effort to God as a small gift of gratitude.

Prayer

Lord, help me to see my daily work as a chance to honor You. Give me energy and a sincere heart for every task I handle today. May everything I do reflect Your love and grace. Amen.
