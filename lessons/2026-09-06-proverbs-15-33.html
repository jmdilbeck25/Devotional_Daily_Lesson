Humility Before True Honor

"Wisdom’s instruction is to fear the Lord, and humility comes before honor." — Proverbs 15:33

In a world that constantly encourages us to promote ourselves, God calls you to a completely different rhythm. True wisdom begins when you acknowledge who God is, honoring His greatness and placing Him at the center of your daily life. When you hold Him in deep respect, the pressure to prove yourself begins to melt away.

Humility is not about putting yourself down; it is about looking up to God and turning your focus toward others. God promises that when you walk softly and stay teachable, He takes care of your reputation. The path to lasting honor always starts on your knees.

Living It Out

Today, look for one quiet opportunity to put someone else's needs ahead of your own without seeking recognition. Whether it is letting someone else take the credit, listening carefully without interrupting, or performing a hidden act of service, practice the gentle posture of humility.

Prayer

Lord, help me to lay down my pride and trust You with my life today. Teach me to walk with a gentle and humble heart that honors You above all else. Amen.
