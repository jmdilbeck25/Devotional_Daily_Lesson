Training Your Heart for Eternity

"For physical training is of some value, but godliness has value for all things, holding promise for both the present life and the life to come." — 1 Timothy 4:8

We spend a lot of time taking care of our bodies. From eating well to squeezing in a daily walk, caring for physical health is helpful, and God cares about every part of us. Physical exercise gives us energy and strength for our day-to-day responsibilities. Yet, as valuable as fitness is, its benefits are limited to our time here on earth.

Godliness, on the other hand, shapes everything. When you nurture your relationship with God through prayer, Scripture, and daily trust, you invest in something that transforms your life now and carries into eternity. While physical strength eventually fades, spiritual growth builds joy, peace, and faith that time can never erode.

Living It Out

Today, set aside five minutes to give your soul a focused workout. Pause your busy routine, open a psalm, and speak honestly with God about your day, asking Him to align your heart with His priorities.

Prayer

Lord, thank You for the gift of my body and the energy You provide each day. Teach me to value my spiritual health even more, turning my heart toward You in every moment. Help me build daily habits of faith that will bless my life today and for eternity. Amen.
