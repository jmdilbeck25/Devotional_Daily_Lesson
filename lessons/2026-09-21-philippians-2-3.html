DAILY DEVOTIONAL

Putting Others First Today

"Do nothing out of selfish ambition or vain conceit. Rather, in humility value others above yourselves," — Philippians 2:3

In a world that constantly encourages you to promote yourself and put your own desires first, the apostle Paul offers a refreshing and radical alternative. True peace and joyful relationships do not come from striving to be superior or fighting for recognition. Instead, they flow from a humble heart that freely chooses to see the immense value in the people around you. When you intentionally set aside selfish ambition, you open up space to reflect the very character of Jesus. Humility is not about thinking less of yourself, but about thinking of yourself less. Today, you are invited to look at those in your life through God's eyes of grace, recognizing that every person you encounter is worthy of love, care, and honor.

Living It Out

Identify one person in your home, workplace, or community today whom you usually overlook or disagree with, and perform a small, intentional act of kindness or encouragement for them without expecting anything in return.

Prayer

Lord, help me to lay down my pride and self-centered desires today. Teach me to see others through Your eyes and to value them above myself. Give me a humble heart that reflects Your love in everything I do. Amen.
