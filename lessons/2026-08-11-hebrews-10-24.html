Spurring Each Other Toward Goodness

"And let us consider how we may spur one another on toward love and good deeds," — Hebrews 10:24

Life in community is not an accident; it is essential to your walk with God. The writer of Hebrews invites you to intentionally think about how you can encourage those around you. To spur someone on means to gently motivate and stir up the good inside them, helping them live out the love Christ has shown us.

Left on your own, it is easy to grow tired, discouraged, or distracted. But when you actively look for ways to lift up others, you create an environment filled with grace and active kindness. Your thoughtful words might be the exact spark someone needs to keep going today.

Living It Out

Reach out to one person in your life today through a text message, phone call, or brief conversation, and tell them specifically how you appreciate them or where you see God working in their life.

Prayer

Lord, open my eyes to the people around me who need encouragement today. Give me the right words to stir up love and kindness in others. Amen.
