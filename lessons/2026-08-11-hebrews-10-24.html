DAILY DEVOTIONAL

Spurring One Another Toward Love

"And let us consider how we may spur one another on toward love and good deeds," — Hebrews 10:24

Life can often feel isolating, and it is easy to get caught up in our own daily routines and struggles. But the writer of Hebrews reminds us that we were never meant to walk this faith journey alone. Notice the word consider—it means to think carefully, intentionally, and creatively about the people around you. It takes deliberate thought to notice who needs a boost and how best to lift them up.

The scripture talks about spurring one another on toward love and good deeds. Sometimes we all need a gentle nudge to keep going, to choose kindness when we are tired, or to serve when we feel distracted. When you actively look out for others, you help create a warm community where God's love is lived out every day.

Living It Out

Today, take five minutes to intentionally consider one person in your life who might be feeling weary. Send them a quick text message or leave a kind note to let them know you care and appreciate them.

Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for placing people in my life to walk alongside me. Help me to be observant today and show me how I can encourage someone else toward love and kindness. Give me the right words and a heart ready to serve. Amen.
