Walking Together in Strength

"Two are better than one, because they have a good return for their labor:" — Ecclesiastes 4:9

It is easy to buy into the idea that we should handle everything on our own. Our culture often celebrates independence, convincing us that asking for help is a sign of weakness. Yet God designed us from the very beginning to live and work in community. When we try to carry every burden alone, we quickly become exhausted and vulnerable to discouragement.

King Solomon reminds us that partnership changes the dynamic of our daily efforts. Working alongside another person does not just lighten the physical or emotional load; it actually multiplies what we can accomplish. God uses our relationships to bring encouragement, wisdom, and fruitfulness that we could never achieve by standing alone.

Living It Out

Today, reach out to a friend, coworker, or family member and invite them to share in a task or conversation you have been carrying alone. Allow yourself to receive support instead of doing everything yourself.

Prayer

Lord, thank you for creating me to live in community rather than isolation. Help me to lay down my pride and welcome others into my daily life and work. Show me who I can partner with today so we can bring glory to you together. Amen.
