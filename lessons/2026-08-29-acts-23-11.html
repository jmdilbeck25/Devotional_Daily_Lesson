The Lord Stands Beside You

"The following night the Lord stood near Paul and said, 'Take courage! As you have testified about me in Jerusalem, so you must also testify in Rome.'" — Acts 23:11

Paul was facing an uncertain future, locked in a cell after violent riots and death threats. In the middle of his darkest, most exhausting night, Jesus did not send a distant message. He came and stood right beside him, giving him comfort and clarity for the road ahead.

You might feel stuck in a difficult season today, wondering if your current struggle will consume you. The same Savior who stood by Paul stands near you right now. He sees your weariness, values your faithfulness, and assures you that your present trial is not the end of your story.

Living It Out

Today, take five minutes to sit quietly and name one specific burden that is causing you anxiety. Consciously hand it over to Jesus in prayer, speaking out loud the promise that He is present with you right now and will guide your next steps.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank You for standing near me even when I feel lonely or overwhelmed. Give me the courage to trust that You hold my future and that my story is not over. Help me to step forward today with confidence in Your presence. Amen.
