Love That Stands the Test

"A friend loves at all times, and a brother is born for a time of adversity." — Proverbs 17:17

True friendship is one of God's sweetest gifts to us. It is easy to stand by someone when life is bright and full of laughter, but real love reveals itself when the storms roll in. Solomon reminds us that genuine friends do not run away during difficult seasons. Instead, hard times draw true companions closer, transforming ordinary bonds into deep, family-like loyalty.

Jesus modeled this perfect, unwavering love for you. He drew near to the brokenhearted, stayed loyal to His followers through their weakest moments, and called us His friends. When you experience trials, God often uses the steady presence of faithful friends to remind you that you are never alone in the struggle.

Living It Out

Reach out to one friend today who is currently navigating a difficult season. Send them a simple message of encouragement, offer a practical act of help, or take a few minutes to listen to them without trying to fix everything.

Prayer

Lord, thank You for the faithful friends You have placed in my life. Help me to be a steady, loving presence to others when they face hard times. Teach me to love like Jesus through every season of life. Amen.
