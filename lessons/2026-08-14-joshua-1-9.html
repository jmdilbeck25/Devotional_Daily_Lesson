You Never Walk Alone

"Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the Lord your God will be with you wherever you go." — Joshua 1:9

Facing new seasons or uncertain steps can feel overwhelming. When God called Joshua to lead Israel, Joshua was facing massive responsibility and unknown territory. God did not tell Joshua to be strong because Joshua possessed all the answers or natural bravery. Instead, God commanded strength and courage based on one essential promise: His abiding presence.

Fear and discouragement often creep in when you focus on your own limitations or the size of your obstacles. But God's presence changes everything. Wherever you go today—into a difficult conversation, a new responsibility, or a quiet room of lingering worries—you are never left alone. God is already there, offering you His unshakeable strength.

Living It Out

Identify one situation today that makes you feel anxious or hesitant. Before you step into it, pause for a moment, take a deep breath, and quietly whisper, Lord, You are with me right here. Let that simple truth guide your next step.

Prayer

Lord, thank You for promising to be with me wherever I go today. Help me to trade my fear and discouragement for Your courage. Remind me of Your faithful presence every step of the way. Amen.
