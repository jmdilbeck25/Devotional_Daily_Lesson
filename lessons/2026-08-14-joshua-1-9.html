God Goes Wherever You Go

"Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the Lord your God will be with you wherever you go." — Joshua 1:9

Taking a new step or facing an unexpected challenge can make anyone feel unprepared. When Joshua was called to lead Israel after Moses died, he faced massive responsibility and daunting obstacles. God did not promise Joshua that the path would be easy or without trouble. Instead, God offered a far better promise: His constant, unwavering presence.

Courage is not the absence of fear, but the certainty that you do not walk alone. Whatever situation you are facing right now, God is already there. You do not have to generate strength on your own because the Creator of the universe walks beside you in every moment and through every trial.

Living It Out

Identify one situation causing you anxiety today. Pause, take a deep breath, and remind yourself that God is actively present with you in that exact moment.

Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for promising to never leave my side. Help me to lay down my fear and lean into Your strength today. Amen.
